Theme 2026

Edge of Equilibrium

Balancing freedom, growth and security – three forces that often pull in different directions. Two days of keynotes, panels and workshops around one question: where is the balance?

  1. I

    Freedom

    Open markets, free societies and individual choice – where freedom creates value, and where it reaches its limits.

  2. II

    Growth

    Innovation, capital and competitiveness – what it takes to grow in a changing economy.

  3. III

    Security

    Geopolitics, defence and resilience – how economies and societies stay secure.
